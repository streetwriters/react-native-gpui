All upstream props work as documented in the [React Native TouchableWithoutFeedback reference](https://reactnative.dev/docs/0.87/touchablewithoutfeedback). This page lists only what desktop adds or changes.

## Desktop props

None. This page has no props of its own on desktop.

## Differences

- `hitSlop` and `pressRetentionOffset` are applied to the pointer.
- `accessibilityLanguage`, `aria-live` and `aria-modal` pass to the accessibility tree.
